Terms & Conditions
How to play
  1. Each Telegram account gets 1 run per day, resetting at 00:00 (UTC+8).
  2. A run lasts 60 seconds of play and starts with 3 lives.
  3. Every tap hops the chicken one lane forward. Each new lane crossed scores 1 point.
  4. Getting hit costs 1 life. You respawn at the last grass checkpoint — every 10th lane — after a 3-second countdown, then briefly invincible.
  5. The clock is paused during every countdown, so a death costs you ground, not time.
  6. Lanes you have already crossed do not score again, so a death costs you real ground.
  7. The run ends when lives run out or time expires. Your score is the furthest lane reached.
  8. Traffic gets faster and denser every 10 lanes.
  9. The leaderboard resets every day at 00:00 (UTC+8). Your rank is your single run for that day — a bad day never follows you.
Same road for everyone
  1. Traffic is generated from a single daily seed. Every player on a given day faces the identical road — same cars, same speeds, same gaps.
  2. The seed is issued by the server at the start of your run and shown in the corner of the screen.
  3. No player receives easier or harder traffic than another.
Invite bonus
  1. Share your personal invite link. Friends who open it subscribe to @KRminigame_bot.
  2. A referral only counts once that friend has played at least one run.
  3. 3 friends +1 life · 6 friends +2 lives · 9 friends +3 lives.
  4. +3 is the cap. Inviting beyond 9 adds nothing further.
  5. Extra lives give you more room for error. They do not add points — distance is still earned by skill.
  6. Applies to every run, counted from your valid total at the moment the run starts.
  7. Referrals are counted for life — they never reset. Each person can only ever be referred once.
  8. Duplicate IPs, duplicate devices, blank new accounts and bulk-added members are excluded.
Rewards & claiming
  1. Top 1 — 30 Credit · Top 2 — 20 Credit · Top 3 — 10 Credit · Top 4 and 5 — 5 Credit.
  2. Tap CLAIM on your row once the day has settled.
  3. On a tie, whoever reached the score first ranks higher — the time each score was set is shown on the board.
  4. Winners are announced daily after the 00:00 reset, shown by Telegram username.
  5. Reward Credit carries a 3× turnover requirement and cannot be withdrawn or transferred until it is met.
  6. Winners must tap CLAIM NOW within 7 days of the announcement. Unclaimed rewards are forfeited.
  7. Credit is issued to your kgame99 account, so registration is required first.
Fair play
  1. The score your device displays is provisional. Only your input timings are submitted; the server replays the run against the daily seed and the server result is final.
  2. Any run whose inputs do not reproduce the claimed score is voided.
  3. Runs showing reaction times consistently below the human threshold are treated as scripted and voided.
  4. Auto-clickers, bots, emulators and multiple accounts forfeit eligibility and freeze any reward.
  5. Full input logs are retained, so any disputed run can be replayed on request.
  6. The organiser reserves the right of final interpretation and may amend these terms at any time.
